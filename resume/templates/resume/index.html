How to Write Each Section

Follow these guidelines to build a strong, professional resume.

📝

Summary

Highlight your best skills and include a concrete example or two of what you have accomplished in your career. Keep it to 3-4 focused sentences that make employers want to read more.

⚡

Skills

Include your proficiency in programming languages, technical tools, and soft skills here. List at least 8 relevant skills separated by commas. Mix technical and interpersonal skills.

💼

Experience

State your tasks and accomplishments at each role including the company name and job title. Use strong action verbs and include measurable results such as numbers, percentages, and outcomes.

🎓

Education

Summarize your academic qualifications by mentioning the university or institution name and the degree studied. Include your graduation year and GPA if it is 3.5 or above.
